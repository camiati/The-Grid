# NISPOM

The National Industrial Security Program Operating Manual (NISPOM)
- codified at 32 CFR Part 117.
- The rules cleared contractors follow to protect classified information, overseen for DoD by the Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency (DCSA).

*Last reviewed October 2026.*

## Key sections

| Section | Covers |
|---|---|
| §&nbsp;117.7 | Procedures: security officials, training, self-inspections, insider threat program |
| §&nbsp;117.8 | Reporting requirements, including SEAD 3 |
| §&nbsp;117.9 | Entity eligibility (facility clearances) |
| §&nbsp;117.10 | Personnel eligibility (personnel clearances) |
| §&nbsp;117.11 | Foreign ownership, control, or influence (FOCI) |
| §&nbsp;117.18 | Classified information systems |

## Practitioner notes

**It's a regulation, not a manual.** 
- The NISPOM became federal rule 32 CFR Part 117 on February 24, 2021, replacing DoD 5220.22-M.
- Older guidance citing manual paragraphs needs translating; DCSA and CDSE publish a cross-reference tool.

**Leadership owns it.**
- The senior management official appoints the FSO and insider threat program senior official in writing and certifies the annual self-inspection.
- Make sure your SMO knows that's a personal responsibility.

**Self-inspections are annual and certified.** 
- Run one at least yearly and keep the written certification.
- CDSE's self-inspection resources are the standard starting point.

**Build reporting into normal business.**
- SEAD 3 reporting (foreign travel, foreign contacts, adverse information) works best as part of onboarding, travel booking, and annual briefings, not as a separate process people forget.

**Fundraising can trigger FOCI.**
- New investors, ownership changes, and board changes can be changed conditions requiring a report and possibly FOCI review.
- Bring the FSO in before a deal closes, not after.

**NISPOM is for classified, not CUI.**
- The rule states CUI compliance is outside the NISP's scope.
- CUI falls under DFARS 7012, NIST SP 800-171, and CMMC, with different assessors. See those pages in this guide.

**Get your PKI or ECA certificate early.**
- DCSA's industry systems require one, and it can take weeks.
- It's the same kind of certificate needed to report DFARS 7012 cyber incidents.

**Continuous vetting changes the FSO's job.**
- Under Trusted Workforce 2.0, cleared personnel are enrolled in continuous vetting.
- FSOs manage enrollment status and FBI Rap Back advisements, not periodic reinvestigation dates.

**Systems are in transition.**
- DCSA's industry systems for facility and personnel clearances are mid-modernization.
- Check DCSA's monthly Voice of Industry newsletter for current instructions.

**Get approval before going public.**
- Public disclosure of information about classified contracts needs government review first.
- That includes press releases, marketing, conference talks, and social media posts.
