# CUI Program

The executive branch program for Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)
- established by Executive Order 13556 and implemented at 32 CFR Part 2002.
- The National Archives (NARA) is the Executive Agent.
- DFARS 7012, NIST SP 800-171, and CMMC all exist to protect what this program defines.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CUI&nbsp;Basic | CUI handled under the default rules in 32 CFR 2002 |
| CUI&nbsp;Specified | CUI where a law or regulation sets specific handling rules, which take precedence |
| CUI&nbsp;Registry | NARA's official list of CUI categories, with authorities and marking requirements |
| DoD&nbsp;CUI&nbsp;Registry | DoD's registry under DoDI 5200.48, aligned to NARA's categories |

## FAR CUI rule (proposed)

| Item | Status |
|---|---|

## Practitioner notes

**Start with whether it's actually CUI.**
- Everything downstream (scope, cost, assessment level) depends on it.
- Check the category against the Registry and your contract, not just the marking on a document.

**Over-marking is common.**
- Government-provided documents are sometimes marked CUI when they shouldn't be, or not marked when they should be.
- When markings look wrong, ask the contracting officer in writing.

**Information you create can be CUI.**
- Data generated under a contract can fall into a CUI category even if nothing you received was marked.
- Know what your contract says about this.

**The Defense category decides your CMMC level.**
- CUI in the CUI Registry's Defense Organizational Index Grouping generally points to Level 2 certification; other CUI can allow self-assessment.
- Third-party requirements are suspended for now; see the CMMC page for current status and the Level Determination brief.

**FOUO isn't CUI.**
- Legacy markings like FOUO and SBU were replaced by the CUI program.
- Treat them as a prompt to determine the correct CUI category.

**Watch for two baselines.**
- DoD contracts still require NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2, while the proposed FAR rule points to Rev. 3.
- Companies with both DoD and civilian contracts may end up tracking both.

**CUI and classified are separate regimes.**
- NISPOM covers classified information and explicitly excludes CUI.
- See the NISPOM page in this guide.
